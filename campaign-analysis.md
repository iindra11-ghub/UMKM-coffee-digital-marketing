Digital Marketing Campaign Analysis

Campaign Overview

Product: Americano
Proposed Price: Rp15,000
Target Audience: Students aged 18–24
Platform: TikTok

Market Insight

The competitor research found Americano prices ranging from Rp18,000 to Rp25,000 at three coffee shops.

The proposed price of Rp15,000 is lower than the prices collected during the research.

Marketing Hypothesis

An affordable price combined with relatable TikTok content may attract students’ attention and encourage interest in the product.

Success Measurement

Primary KPI: Views within 7 days.

Target: 1,000 views within 7 days of publishing.

Next Evaluation

After publishing, compare actual views with the target. Use the results to decide what to improve in the next video.

Limitations

Competitor prices need to be verified before making business decisions. The campaign hypothesis has not yet been tested, and no actual performance results are available.

Practice: Click-Through Rate (CTR)

Simulated Data

* Impressions: 1,000
* Link Clicks: 40

Calculation

CTR = Link Clicks / Impressions × 100%

CTR = 40 / 1,000 × 100%

Result: 4%

Insight

CTR measures the percentage of impressions that resulted in link clicks. A higher CTR may indicate that the content or call to action encourages more people to click.

Note: These figures are simulated for learning purposes, not actual campaign results.
