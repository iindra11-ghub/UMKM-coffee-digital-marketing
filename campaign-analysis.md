# Digital Marketing Campaign Analysis

## 1. Campaign Overview

- **Product:** Americano
- **Proposed Price:** Rp15,000
- **Target Audience:** Students aged 18–24
- **Platform:** TikTok
- **Campaign Objective:** Increase awareness and attract student interest.

## 2. Market Insight

The competitor research found Americano prices ranging from Rp18,000 to Rp25,000 at three coffee shops.

The proposed price of Rp15,000 is lower than the prices collected during the research.

This creates a marketing opportunity to attract students who are looking for affordable coffee.

## 3. Marketing Hypothesis

An affordable price combined with relatable TikTok content may attract students' attention and encourage interest in the product.

## 4. KPI and Success Measurement

**Primary KPI:** Video Views

**Target:** 1,000 views within 7 days of publishing.

Supporting metrics:
- Average Watch Time
- Engagement Rate
- Profile Visits

These metrics help evaluate audience attention, interaction, and interest in the coffee shop.

## 5. Practice: KPI Analysis

### Simulated Results

- Target: 1,000 views in 7 days
- Actual Views: 750
- Target Achievement: 75%

### Calculation

Target Achievement = Actual Views / Target Views × 100%

= 750 / 1,000 × 100%

= 75%

### Insight

The simulated result reached 75% of the target, leaving a gap of 250 views.

A possible improvement is to test a stronger opening hook in the next video.

Views alone cannot explain why the target was missed. Additional data, such as average watch time and audience retention, would help evaluate content performance.

## 6. Practice: Engagement Rate

### Simulated Data

- Views: 750
- Likes: 45
- Comments: 5
- Total Interactions: 50

### Calculation

Engagement Rate = (Likes + Comments) / Views × 100%

= 50 / 750 × 100%

= 6.67%

### Insight

This metric measures how many viewers interacted with the content relative to total views.

A higher engagement rate may indicate stronger audience interaction.

## 7. Practice: Click-Through Rate (CTR)

### Simulated Data

- Impressions: 1,000
- Link Clicks: 40

### Calculation

CTR = Link Clicks / Impressions × 100%

= 40 / 1,000 × 100%

= 4%

### Insight

CTR measures the percentage of impressions that resulted in link clicks.

A higher CTR may indicate that the content or call to action encourages more people to click.

## 8. Practice: Comparing Content Performance

### Simulated Data

| Metric | Video A | Video B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Views | 1,000 | 800 |
| Likes | 40 | 64 |
| Like Rate | 4% | 8% |

### Analysis

Video B received fewer views but achieved a higher like rate than Video A.

This suggests that Video B generated stronger interaction relative to its views.

### Recommendation

Investigate what made Video B more engaging. Test its hook or content approach in a future video and compare the results.

## 9. A/B Testing Plan: TikTok Hook

### Objective

Compare two opening hooks to identify which one attracts more attention from students aged 18–24.

### Version A

"Nongkrong di kafe nggak harus mahal."

### Version B

"Cuma punya 15 ribu, masih bisa ngopi di kafe?"

### Hypothesis

Version B may attract more attention because it mentions a specific budget and creates curiosity.

### Measurement

- Primary Metric: Average Watch Time
- Supporting Metrics: Views and Engagement Rate
- Comparison Period: 7 days

### Testing Conditions

Keep the video footage, duration, offer, and call to action as similar as possible.

Only change the opening hook to make the comparison more meaningful.

### Data Status

This is a proposed experiment. No actual A/B testing results have been collected.
