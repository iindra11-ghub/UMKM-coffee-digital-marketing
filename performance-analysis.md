# Coffee Shop Marketing Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This document contains a marketing performance analysis based on simulated data from a local coffee shop digital marketing project.

The purpose is to understand campaign performance, evaluate marketing strategies, and identify opportunities for improvement.

## Performance Summary

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Views | 6,500 |
| Total Purchases | 130 |
| Average Engagement Rate | 7.2% |
| Average Conversion Rate | 3% |
| Total Revenue | Rp1,950,000 |

## Important Note

All figures in this project are simulated for learning and portfolio purposes. They do not represent actual campaign results.

## Marketing Insights

1. The campaign generated 6,500 total views, showing the potential to reach the target audience.

2. A total of 130 purchases were recorded in the simulation, indicating that the campaign can generate customer interest.

3. The average engagement rate was 7.2%, which can be used to evaluate audience interaction.

4. The next strategy should focus on improving content quality, audience retention, and conversion performance.

## Recommendations

- Test different video hooks to improve audience retention.
- Develop content based on student interests and affordable coffee prices.
- Evaluate conversion rates to understand campaign effectiveness.
- Monitor marketing costs and revenue to improve profitability.
