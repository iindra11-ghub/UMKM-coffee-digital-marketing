# Marketing Funnel Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This document analyzes a simulated marketing funnel for a local coffee shop targeting students aged 18–24.

The objective is to understand how potential customers move from discovering the business to making a purchase.

## 2. Simulated Funnel Data

| Customer Journey Stage | Number of People |
|---|---:|
| TikTok Views | 10,000 |
| Instagram Profile Visits | 1,000 |
| Direct Messages | 500 |
| Purchases | 50 |

## 3. Conversion Rate Analysis

### Views to Profile Visits

Conversion Rate = 1,000 / 10,000 × 100%

Result: 10%

### Profile Visits to Direct Messages

Conversion Rate = 500 / 1,000 × 100%

Result: 50%

### Direct Messages to Purchases

Conversion Rate = 50 / 500 × 100%

Result: 10%

### Overall Conversion Rate

Conversion Rate = 50 / 10,000 × 100%

Result: 0.5%

## 4. Funnel Analysis

The simulated data shows that:

- 10% of TikTok viewers visited the Instagram profile.
- 50% of profile visitors sent a direct message.
- 10% of people who sent a direct message completed a purchase.

The DM-to-purchase stage may need further evaluation because only 10% of people who made inquiries completed a purchase.

## 5. Proposed Improvements

Possible improvements include:

- Providing clear menu and price information.
- Improving admin response time.
- Sharing customer reviews and product information.
- Providing clear directions and location details.

These improvements aim to help potential customers make purchasing decisions.

## 6. Project Conclusion

Marketing funnel analysis helps businesses understand customer behavior at each stage of the buying journey.

By measuring conversion rates, businesses can identify potential obstacles and develop more effective marketing strategies.

## 7. Data Limitations

All figures in this analysis are simulated for learning purposes.

They are not actual campaign results or verified customer transaction data.
