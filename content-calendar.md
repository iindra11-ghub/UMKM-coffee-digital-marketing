# TikTok Content Calendar

## Campaign: Affordable Americano

| Day | Content Idea | Hook | Objective |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | POV menikmati kopi di tengah kesibukan | POV: Orang lain sibuk kerja, lu menikmati kopi | Increase brand awareness |
| Day 2 | Informasi harga Americano | Kopi enak nggak harus mahal | Highlight affordability |
| Day 3 | Nongkrong bareng teman | Nongkrong bareng teman, tetap bisa hemat! | Encourage engagement |
| Day 4 | Suasana coffee shop | Butuh tempat buat santai setelah kuliah? | Build brand awareness |
| Day 5 | Rekomendasi kopi untuk mahasiswa | Budget terbatas, tapi pengen ngopi? | Attract student audience |

## Publishing Plan

- **Platform:** TikTok
- **Format:** Short-form video
- **Primary KPI:** Views
- **Measurement:** Record views 24 hours and 7 days after publishing.
- **Target Audience:** University students aged 18–24.

## Data Status

This is a proposed content plan for a learning portfolio. The content ideas are planning concepts, not published campaigns. Actual publishing dates and performance results have not been recorded.
