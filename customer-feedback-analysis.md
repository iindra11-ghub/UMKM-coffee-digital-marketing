# Customer Feedback Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This document explains how a coffee shop can collect and analyze customer feedback to improve customer satisfaction.

The target audience is students aged 18–24.

## 2. Customer Feedback Methods

The coffee shop can collect customer feedback through:

- Suggestion boxes near the cashier.
- Online surveys using Google Forms.
- Direct customer reviews.
- Social media messages.

## 3. Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)

CSAT is a metric used to measure customer satisfaction with a product or service.

### Example Survey

Question:

"How satisfied are you with your experience at our coffee shop?"

Rating scale:
- 1: Very dissatisfied
- 2: Dissatisfied
- 3: Neutral
- 4: Satisfied
- 5: Very satisfied

## 4. Simulated CSAT Calculation

### Survey Results

- Total respondents: 100
- Satisfied customers (rating 4–5): 85
- Dissatisfied or neutral customers (rating 1–3): 15

### Calculation

CSAT = Satisfied Customers / Total Respondents × 100%

CSAT = 85 / 100 × 100%

Result: 85%

### Insight

The simulated CSAT score is 85%, meaning 85% of respondents rated their experience as satisfied or very satisfied.

## 5. Customer Feedback Analysis

Example customer complaints:

| Customer Complaint | Number of Responses |
|---|---:|
| Slow service | 20 |
| Expensive prices | 10 |
| Inconsistent drink quality | 5 |

The most frequently reported issue is slow service.

Therefore, the coffee shop should prioritize evaluating its service process.

## 6. Proposed Improvements

Possible improvements include:

- Improving employee service efficiency.
- Reviewing product quality consistency.
- Evaluating menu prices.
- Collecting feedback regularly.

## 7. Feedback Loop

The business can follow these steps:

1. Collect customer feedback.
2. Analyze common complaints.
3. Prioritize the most important issues.
4. Implement improvements.
5. Measure customer satisfaction again.

## 8. Project Conclusion

Customer feedback helps businesses understand customer expectations and identify areas for improvement.

Regular CSAT measurement can help evaluate whether business improvements lead to better customer satisfaction.
