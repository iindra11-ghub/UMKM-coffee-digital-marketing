# Customer Journey Analysis

## Project Overview

This document analyzes the customer journey of a local coffee shop targeting students aged 18–24.

The objective is to understand how potential customers discover, evaluate, and purchase coffee products.

## 1. Awareness

**Customer Behavior:**
Students discover the coffee shop through TikTok content.

**Marketing Strategy:**
Create engaging videos showing affordable coffee prices, cafe atmosphere, and product highlights.

**KPI:**
Video Views and Reach.

## 2. Interest

**Customer Behavior:**
Students become interested and visit the coffee shop's Instagram profile.

**Marketing Strategy:**
Provide clear information about the menu, prices, location, and cafe atmosphere.

**KPI:**
Profile Visits.

## 3. Consideration

**Customer Behavior:**
Potential customers compare prices, ask questions, and consider visiting.

**Marketing Strategy:**
Share menu information, customer reviews, and promotional offers.

**KPI:**
Direct Messages and Menu Inquiries.

## 4. Conversion

**Customer Behavior:**
Customers visit the coffee shop and make a purchase.

**Marketing Strategy:**
Provide clear product information, attractive offers, and responsive customer service.

**KPI:**
Number of Transactions.

## 5. Retention

**Customer Behavior:**
Customers return to purchase again.

**Marketing Strategy:**
Introduce loyalty programs, collect customer feedback, and maintain product quality.

**KPI:**
Repeat Purchase Rate.

## Project Conclusion

Understanding the customer journey helps businesses identify customer needs at every stage.

By evaluating each stage, a coffee shop can improve its marketing strategy and customer experience.

## Data Status

This customer journey is a proposed strategy for a learning portfolio project. It has not been validated using actual customer data.
