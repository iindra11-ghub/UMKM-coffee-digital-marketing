
# Competitor Research — Coffee Shop

## Research Objective

To compare Americano prices at selected coffee shops and understand the potential price positioning for a student-focused coffee campaign.

## Competitor Price Comparison

| Coffee Shop     | Americano Price |
|-----------------------------------|
| Lobby           | Rp25,000        |
| House of Malaga | Rp18,000        |
| Kolam           | Rp19,000        |

Note: These prices were collected during preliminary research and have not yet been independently verified.

## Price Analysis

- Highest recorded price: Rp25,000
- Lowest recorded price: Rp18,000
- Average recorded price: Approximately Rp20,667

## Proposed Price Positioning

The proposed Americano price of Rp15,000 is approximately 27.4% below the average recorded competitor price of Rp20,667.

This price may appeal to budget-conscious students. However, the proposed price should not be finalized until product costs, profit margins, and customer demand have been evaluated.

### Business Considerations

- Calculate the cost per cup before setting the final price.
- Check whether the price can generate a sustainable profit.
- Validate customer interest before assuming that a lower price will increase sales.

Note: The proposed price is a planning assumption, not a confirmed offer.


## Initial Marketing Insight

A proposed Americano price of Rp15,000 would be lower than all three recorded competitor prices.

This could be an attractive price point for students, but customer demand and business profitability have not been tested.

## Limitations

- Prices must be verified before making business decisions.
- The research covers only three coffee shops.
- Product sizes, ingredients, and promotions may differ between competitors.
- No customer survey has been conducted.

## Next Steps

- Verify competitor prices and serving sizes.
- Research what students value when choosing a coffee shop.
- Use the findings to develop a realistic pricing and content strategy.

## Data Status

This is preliminary competitor research for a learning portfolio. Findings and recommendations should be validated before being used for actual business decisions.
